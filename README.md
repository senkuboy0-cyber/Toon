# Cloudstream Extension: Tooniboy

A **Cloudstream** extension dedicated to streaming anime, cartoons, and movies from **Tooniboy** with high-quality multi-language audio, Hindi dubbing, and subtitle support.

---

## Features

- **Hindi & Multi-Language** Dubbed Anime and Cartoons
- **Anime Series & Movies** Streaming
- **Multiple Server Support** (10 Servers)
- **Search Functionality**
- **Subtitle Support**

---

## Supported Sources

| Source | Language | Type |
|--------|----------|------|
| [Tooniboy](https://tooniboy.co/) | Hindi & Multi-Language | Anime, Movies, Series, Cartoon |

---

## Servers

VidStreamX (Zephyrflick), Abyss, StreamRuby, Cloudy, GDMirrorbot (SD/HD/FHD), EmTurboVid, VidMolyNet, Blakite (MultiQ)

---

## Requirements

- **Cloudstream3** app installed on your device
- Android 5.0+ (API 21+)
